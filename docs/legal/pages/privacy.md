# Privacy Policy

**Translita**

**Last updated: 9 October 2026**

## The short version

Translita does not ask you to create an account and does not ask who you are.

Your translations, your history and your settings stay on your phone. Three things leave it, and only when you use the feature that needs them: text you choose to translate online, speech you dictate, and sentences you speak to the AI practice partner. When you open the app it also sends our server a short wake-up request, which contains nothing about you.

We do not sell your data.

## Who we are

Translita is published by **Translita Offline Translator**, of **Attock, Pakistan**.

For any privacy question, write to **devopsmarkaz@gmail.com**.

## What stays on your phone

All of the following is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot read it, and it is never uploaded:

- Your translation history
- Your Conversation and AI practice transcripts, so you can pick them up again later
- Your saved phrases and their translations
- Your language choices, theme, voice and speech-rate settings
- Downloaded offline language packs
- Whether you have completed the welcome screens, and how many free AI practice replies you have used

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## What leaves your phone, and when

### Text you translate online

When your device is online and you translate, the text you typed, scanned or spoke is sent to our server, which passes it to **Microsoft Azure AI Translator** and returns the translation.

- We do not store the text. It is held in memory for the length of the request and then discarded.
- Azure processes it under Microsoft's terms. See the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).
- **If you translate offline, nothing is sent anywhere.** The translation happens entirely on your device.

### A wake-up request

When you open Translita, or come back to it, the app sends a short request to our server so it is ready before your first online translation. The request contains nothing about you and nothing you have typed — it only asks the server whether it is running. It is not sent while Translita is set to translate offline only, or when your phone has no connection.

### Usage statistics

To understand which features people use, Translita sends usage statistics to **Google Analytics for Firebase**: which screens and features are used (for example, opening Conversation or the Pro screen, or making a translation), the language codes of a translation (such as "en" to "de"), and whether the app is on the free or Pro plan. Firebase also collects an identifier for the app installation, the device model, the Android version and an approximate country. It never receives the text you type, scan or speak, or your saved phrases. See [how Google uses data from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### Speech you dictate

Dictation uses your phone's own speech recogniser. On most Android phones this is supplied by Google and **sends your audio to Google's servers** to transcribe it.

That happens between your phone and Google. The audio never reaches us. It is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and by your device settings.

### AI practice conversations

If you use the AI practice partner, the sentence you spoke and the recent exchanges in that conversation are sent to our server, which passes them to **OpenAI** to generate a reply.

- We do not store the conversation. It is held for the length of the request.
- OpenAI processes it under their API terms. See [OpenAI's API data usage policies](https://openai.com/policies/api-data-usage-policies).
- Practice conversations are **not** saved to your translation history. The transcript is kept only on your phone, on the Conversation screen, until you clear it.

### Offline language packs

When you download a language pack, your phone fetches the translation model from **Google's** servers — the offline translator is Google ML Kit. Only the request for the model is sent, never the text you translate. Once a pack is downloaded, offline translation happens entirely on your device.

### Camera

Camera text recognition runs entirely on your device. Pictures are never uploaded, never stored, and are discarded once the text has been read.

## Advertising

The free version of Translita shows adverts supplied by **Google AdMob**.

To show them, AdMob collects information including your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about your device. This is used to select and measure adverts.

- See [how Google uses data from partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in **Android Settings → Privacy → Ads**.
- **Translita Pro removes all adverts**, and with them the advertising SDK's collection.

## Subscriptions

Translita Pro is sold through **Google Play**. We never see or handle your payment details.

Subscription status is managed by **RevenueCat** on our behalf. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier generated for your installation, and your purchase status from Google Play. It does not receive your name or your email address.

- See [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).
- Google Play's terms apply to the payment itself.

## Children

Translita is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided personal information, write to **devopsmarkaz@gmail.com** and we will delete it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, or to object to it being processed.

Because Translita has no accounts, we hold almost nothing tied to you. In practice:

- **Data on your phone** — you can delete it yourself at any time. See _Deleting your data_ below.
- **Advertising identifier** — reset or delete it in Android Settings.
- **Anything else** — write to **devopsmarkaz@gmail.com** and we will respond within 30 days.

## Deleting your data

**In the app:**

- **Settings → Clear translation history** removes your saved translations.
- **Settings → Reset settings** restores every preference to its default.
- **The bin icon on the Conversation screen** clears a saved conversation.
- **Uninstalling the app** removes everything it has stored.

**On our servers:** we keep no account and no stored copy of your translations or practice conversations, so there is nothing on our servers to delete.

**To make a formal request**, see our [Data Deletion page](./data-deletion.md), or email **devopsmarkaz@gmail.com** with the subject "Data deletion".

## Where data is processed

Our server is hosted by **Render**. Azure, OpenAI, Google and RevenueCat process data in their own regions, which may be outside your country — including the United States. Where required, those transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses.

## Security

Traffic between the app and our server is encrypted with HTTPS. Provider credentials are held only on our server and are never included in the app.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will change. Continuing to use Translita after a change means you accept it.
