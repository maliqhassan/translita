# Deleting your Translita data

**Last updated: {{LAST_UPDATED}}**

## Translita has no accounts

There is no sign-in, no email address, no password and no profile. We do not hold an account for you, and we do not store your translations or your practice conversations on our servers.

Almost everything Translita keeps is stored on your own device, and you can delete it yourself at any time.

## Delete your data from the app

| What                         | How                                                      |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Translation history          | **Settings → Clear translation history**                 |
| All settings and preferences | **Settings → Reset settings**                            |
| Downloaded language packs    | **Settings → Download languages**, then delete each pack |
| Everything at once           | **Uninstall the app**                                    |

Uninstalling removes your history, your settings and any downloaded language packs from your device.

## Your advertising identifier

If you use the free version, Google AdMob uses your device's advertising identifier to select and measure adverts. That identifier is held by Google, not by us.

You can reset or delete it at any time in **Android Settings → Privacy → Ads**.

Subscribing to Translita Pro removes all adverts, and with them the advertising SDK's collection.

## Your subscription

If you subscribe to Translita Pro, your purchase is recorded by Google Play and by RevenueCat, which holds an anonymous identifier for your installation and your purchase status.

Manage or cancel your subscription in **Google Play → Subscriptions**. For deletion of purchase records, contact Google Play, who hold them.

## What we hold on our servers

Nothing that identifies you.

- Text you translate online is passed to our translation provider and discarded. It is not stored.
- Sentences you speak to the AI practice partner are passed to our provider and discarded. They are not stored.
- We keep no account, no profile and no usage history tied to you.
